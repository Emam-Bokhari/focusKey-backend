Account Deletion Guide

How to Delete Your Account

Follow the steps below to permanently delete your FocusKey account and all associated data directly inside the mobile app.

  1. Open Settings Tab

    Launch the FocusKey app on your device and tap on the Settings icon located on the bottom right of the main navigation bar.

    FocusKey Settings — Account overview and options
  2. Select "Delete account"

    Under the ACCOUNT section at the top of the Settings screen, tap the red Delete account option.

  3. Confirm with Your Password

    On the confirmation screen, enter your current account Password to verify your ownership and request.

    FocusKey Delete Account — Password verification screen
  4. Permanently Delete Account

    Tap the black Permanently Delete Account button to complete the process. You will be immediately logged out, and your account deletion will take effect permanently.

    Permanent & Irreversible Action
    Deleting your account is permanent. Your focus history, friend connections, paired key status, and custom modes will be vanished into the quietude. This action cannot be undone.
